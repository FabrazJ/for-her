To the Most Extraordinary Girl

It’s honestly funny to think that, through such a strange and crazy turn of events, our paths could have crossed the way they did. We could have easily just been two people who happened to meet, but somehow we ended up getting along really well. And honestly, I can say that it has been 100% worth it.

Since then, I’ve had the chance to learn a little more about your personality, your sense of humor, your curiosity, and, of course, all the random moments that somehow make our conversations more entertaining.

There is also something I genuinely appreciate about you: the version of yourself that you’ve shown and built with me has made me realize that you’re not everything people have said about you. You’re different from the way others may portray you, and even different from the way you sometimes portray yourself. Getting to know you has simply shown me that there’s more to you than any outside impression could ever capture.

You’ve honestly come across as a genuinely kind and thoughtful person. You also have a pretty unpredictable sense of humor, which can make even simple conversations more enjoyable. I appreciate that you can be both straightforward and easy to talk to, while still having your own way of seeing things.

And then there is the path you’ve chosen for yourself.

Never doubt yourself when it comes to becoming an incredible doctor. I hope you remember that you are capable of much more than you sometimes give yourself credit for.

I know medicine will ask a lot from you. There will be long days, difficult exams, moments of exhaustion, and probably a few occasions when you’ll wonder whether all the effort is worth it.

When those moments come, I hope you remember why you started. Not simply because you want a title, but because behind the profession there is something much more meaningful: the opportunity to help people, to make someone feel understood, and to be there during moments that may matter enormously in another person's life.

Knowledge will make you a good doctor. Experience will make you a better one. But empathy, patience, and the way you make people feel cared for are the things that can make you truly unforgettable.

And from the little I’ve been able to discover about you, I believe you already carry some of those qualities with you.

So keep learning. Keep being curious. Keep laughing at the random things that make you, well... you.

Don’t let one difficult day convince you that you are not capable of reaching the future you’re working so hard for. A difficult moment is just that — a moment. It doesn’t get to define your ability, your worth, or everything you are capable of becoming.

I hope that one day, when you finally look back at this part of your life, you’ll realize that every late night, every challenge, every doubt, and every little victory was quietly shaping the doctor you were meant to become.

There’s a small detail I wanted to give you as a way of saying thank you. I’d been meaning to send you these lilies for a while, simply as a small gesture of appreciation.

Back then, I didn't really know what the future held, nor did I know if we’d end up talking, keeping a random TikTok streak going, or—better yet—getting to know so many different sides of you. It was simply a small gesture that felt right.

And now, looking back, it seems strange to me—sending you that bouquet as a surprise—because I had asked you about your favorite roses or flowers last year; it was something I’d planned but never actually followed through on because I felt a bit awkward about it. Just so you know—just to clarify.

With deepest admiration, genuine affection, and my unending support,

M.V
